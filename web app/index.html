Simple Process

From Report to Resolution

Four straightforward steps that connect students to facilities management — no phone calls, no paperwork, no lost emails.

1
Register & Sign In

Create your UNAM student account using your institutional email address to access the full reporting system.

2
Submit Your Report

Fill in the issue form, select a category, provide your location, and attach photos as evidence.

3
Staff Reviews & Acts

The facilities management team receives your report instantly, prioritizes it, and assigns a technician.

4
Track & Confirm

Monitor your report's progress in real time. Confirm resolution once the issue has been fixed to your satisfaction.
